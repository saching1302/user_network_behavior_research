# Figure guide

These figures are designed to support the empirical/pilot portion of the eventual paper.

- Figure 1: establishes the distribution of a key behavioral variable.
- Figure 2: illustrates a behavioral relationship between login and file activity.
- Figure 3: shows the decision-oriented risk categories.
- Figure 4: ranks the highest-risk users for analyst attention.
- Figure 5: compares file activity during business and after-hours categories.

Do not call the synthetic results empirical organizational evidence.
